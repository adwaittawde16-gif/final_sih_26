SLIDE 02/06 — PROPOSED SOLUTION & TECH APPROACH

Proposed Solution & Detailed Technical Approach

The Problem & Solution Vision

The Challenge: CDR call logs, CCTV sightings, and FIR reports reside in isolated data silos. Investigating officers spend weeks manually mapping suspect links during active syndicate operations, leaving high-risk repeat offenders undetected.

Our Solution: A unified Tactical Intelligence Command Center combining FastAPI backend & Next.js 14 frontend that correlates multi-source police data into automated NetworkX call interaction graphs and dynamic 6-parameter threat ratings out of 100.

1. Multi-Modal CDR Graph Mining

Constructs interactive NetworkX graphs, extracts call interaction weights, and maps hidden crime syndicate clusters.

2. Physical CCTV Co-Location Verification

Cross-references call pairs with CCTV timestamps to pinpoint physical encounters (97% match confidence at camera MH-CCTV-2466).

3. 6-Factor Composite Threat Index (0-100)

Scores suspects objectively: CCTV (30), CDR (20), FIR (15), History (15), Nocturnal (10), Financials (10).

4. SHA-256 Cryptographic Hash Chain Audit

Hashes every search query with the prior log entry hash in SQLite, guaranteeing 100% court-admissible audit proof.

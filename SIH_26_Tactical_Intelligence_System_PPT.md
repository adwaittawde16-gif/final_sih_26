### 📍 SLIDE 2: Proposed Solution & Detailed Technical Approach
- **Header:** Core Solution & Innovation
- **Title:** Proposed Solution & Detailed Technical Approach
- **Left Box (The Problem & Solution Pitch):**
  - **The Challenge:** CDRs, CCTV logs, and FIR reports reside in isolated data silos. Manual cross-referencing takes weeks during active syndicate investigations, leaving high-risk repeat offenders undetected.
  - **Our Solution:** A unified Tactical Intelligence Command Center combining FastAPI (Backend) and Next.js 14 (Frontend) that correlates multi-source police data into automated network graphs and dynamic 6-parameter threat ratings out of 100.
- **Right Column (4 Technical Pillars):**
  1. **Multi-Modal CDR Graph Mining:** Interactive NetworkX call interaction graphs auto-detecting hidden crime ring syndicates.
  2. **Physical CCTV Meeting Miner:** Cross-references call pair activity with CCTV camera timestamps to confirm physical encounters (97% match confidence at MH-CCTV-2466).
  3. **6-Factor Composite Threat Index:** Scores suspects (0-100): CCTV (30), CDR (20), FIR (15), History (15), Nocturnal (10), Financials (10).
  4. **SHA-256 Tamper-Evident Audit:** Hashes every query log entry with prior log hash to guarantee 100% court-admissible audit proof.
- **🎤 Speaker Script:**  
  *"Our solution addresses data silos by combining call interaction graphs with physical CCTV meeting timestamps, generating an objective 6-parameter threat score out of 100, and sealing every search query in a SHA-256 tamper-evident cryptographic hash chain."*

### 📍 SLIDE 3: Technical Architecture & Operational Workflow
- **Header:** System Architecture & Flow
- **Title:** Technical Architecture & Operational Workflow
- **Top 3 Architecture Tiers:**
  - **Frontend Dashboard:** Next.js 14 App Router, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet GPS Maps, D3.js Force Graphs.
  - **REST Backend & Engine:** Python FastAPI REST server, Pandas & NetworkX graph engine, 6-Factor Threat Classifier, Regex FIR NLP Parser.
  - **Data & Security Layer:** Structured CSV/JSON Datasets, SQLite SHA-256 Hash Audit Chain, Non-root Docker (UID 1000).
- **Bottom 5 Operational Stages:**
  - **Stage 1 (Ingestion):** Ingests CDR logs, CCTV feeds, FIR databases & financial data.
  - **Stage 2 (Graph Mining):** Maps caller-receiver pairs, nocturnal calls (12AM-6AM), and durations.
  - **Stage 3 (CCTV Meeting):** Verifies frequent callers near the same CCTV camera zone.
  - **Stage 4 (Threat Rating):** Computes 6-factor composite score (0-100) per suspect.
  - **Stage 5 (SHA-256 Audit):** Dispatches alert feed and hashes query to SQLite chain.
- **🎤 Speaker Script:**  
  *"Engineered on a 3-tier modular architecture, our Next.js 14 frontend connects with a FastAPI backend. Our Pandas and NetworkX engines analyze logs in under 100 milliseconds across a 5-stage operational pipeline."*
